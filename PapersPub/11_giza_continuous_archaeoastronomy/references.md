## D. Methodological status

- `Spence2000` is a serious astronomical chronology model, not a settled archaeological date.
- `RawlinsPickering2001`, `Belmonte2001`, and `Wall2007` must be included whenever Spence-style chronology or shaft alignment is discussed; supportive citation without adversarial citation is incomplete.
- `Richardson2013` is the highest-priority source in this set for instrumented shaft exploration.
- `Miatello2020` is useful for geometry and interpretation but its ritual/geometric synthesis is not treated as consensus by this package.
- `Trimble1964` and `Badawy1964` are historically foundational and should be cited for precedence, not rediscovered as new RLL claims.
- `Capitaine2003` and `Vondrak2011/2012` govern modern precession calculation methodology; they do not establish Egyptian intent.
- arXiv and viXra are preprint routes, not peer-review gates.

## E. Citation integrity

Bibliographic facts may be copied as metadata. Substantive prose is paraphrased in the draft. No sentence from a source is presented as original RLL prose. When a direct quotation is ever necessary, it must be short, explicitly quoted, source-bound and copyright-compliant.
