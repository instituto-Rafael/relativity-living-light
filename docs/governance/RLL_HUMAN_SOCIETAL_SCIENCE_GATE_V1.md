# RLL Human / Societal / Science Gate V1

Status: **RESEARCH GOVERNANCE / CLAIM_ALLOWED=false**

## Purpose

RLL may explore mathematics, cosmology, geometry, recurrence, simulation and observational hypotheses. This document prevents a scientifically interesting result from being silently promoted into authority over health, children, education, culture, identity, public policy, privacy or human worth.

```text
MATHEMATICAL_MODEL != PHYSICAL_VALIDATION
PHYSICAL_HYPOTHESIS != CLINICAL_AUTHORITY
COSMOLOGICAL_MODEL != HUMAN_WORTH_MODEL
SCIENTIFIC_NOVELTY != SOCIAL_PERMISSION
```

## Research freedom with claim boundaries

The gate does not prohibit speculative or transdisciplinary research. It requires each result to preserve its actual epistemic state:

```text
FORMAL_MATH
SIMULATION_ONLY
OBSERVED_DATA
REPRODUCED_INTERNAL
EXTERNAL_REPLICATION_TOKEN_VAZIO
HYPOTHESIS
CONTESTED
FALSIFIED
TOKEN_VAZIO
```

A beautiful mathematical correspondence remains publishable as a correspondence without being represented as empirical validation.

## Human-impact boundary

If an RLL result is later used to support a decision about people, the downstream use must create a separate human-impact record. The scientific result itself cannot supply the missing legitimacy.

Material domains include:

- health and mental health;
- children and education;
- personal or sensitive data;
- public safety;
- essential resources;
- civil/human rights;
- culture, belief and identity;
- accessibility/disability;
- environmental and humanitarian impacts.

## Health

```text
RLL_RESULT != DIAGNOSIS
RLL_RESULT != TREATMENT_AUTHORIZATION
```

Any health use requires independent domain evidence/review appropriate to that use. Research code, a fit statistic or an internal reproduction is insufficient clinical authority.

## Children and education

Where a child can be materially affected:

```text
BEST_INTEREST_OF_CHILD = HARD_CONSTRAINT
```

The final high-impact decision must not be exclusively algorithmic. Age, context, safeguarding and applicable expert review remain independent gates.

## Culture, belief and identity

RLL may compare historical, symbolic, geometric or cultural motifs as research context. It must preserve:

```text
CULTURAL_ANALOGY != EMPIRICAL_PROOF
CONTEXT_AWARENESS != PERSONAL_WORTH_RANKING
```

No person’s dignity, truthfulness, intelligence or worth may be scored from culture, belief or identity merely because a model can encode those features.

## Privacy

Personal data are not an automatic extension of an observational dataset. A downstream personal-data use must declare purpose, necessity, minimization, sensitivity, access and retention separately from cosmological/scientific provenance.

```text
DATASET_PRESENCE != PERMISSION_TO_REUSE_PERSONAL_DATA
```

## Public policy and essential resources

A scientific model may inform a deliberation. It must not become sole final authority for a high-impact public allocation or restriction.

The downstream record must expose:

- affected groups;
- benefit and harm distribution;
- unknown risks;
- consequence radius;
- privacy surface;
- review roles;
- appeal or contestation path;
- evidence and falsifier.

## Anti-regression

```text
LATEST != STRONGER
FIT_QUALITY != CAUSAL_TRUTH
PERFORMANCE_GAIN != RIGHTS_OVERRIDE
TOKEN_VAZIO != PASS
```

A successor result cannot upgrade a scientific claim without stronger evidence, and no performance gain can silently weaken a child, privacy, dignity, appeal or human-rights gate.

## Machine-readable contract

`data/governance/RLL_HUMAN_SOCIETAL_SCIENCE_GATE_V1.json`

The structural validator may verify that the boundaries remain present. It cannot certify ethical adequacy, legal compliance, clinical safety, child safety or absence of social/environmental harm.
