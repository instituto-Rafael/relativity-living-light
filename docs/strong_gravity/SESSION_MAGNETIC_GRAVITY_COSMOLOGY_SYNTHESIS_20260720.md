## Falsifiers

The multiscale hypothesis loses support if:

1. standard GRMHD reproduces all observables without an RLL residual;
2. coherence time is shorter than the dynamical time in every plausible regime;
3. mass loading keeps `sigma << 1` where relativistic acceleration is claimed;
4. topology cannot sustain the required flux or current closure;
5. forward-modelled polarization/spectra fail against observations;
6. effects disappear under resolution, coordinate or density-floor tests;
7. population integration yields negligible cosmological contribution;
8. the residual is degenerate with known plasma or instrumental effects.
