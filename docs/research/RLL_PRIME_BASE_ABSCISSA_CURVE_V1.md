## 1. Abscissa

Use the integer sequence itself as the explicit abscissa:

\[
x=n,\qquad n\in\mathbb Z.
\]

For each modulus `m`, define:

\[
r_m(n)=n\bmod m,
\]

\[
\theta_m(n)=2\pi\frac{r_m(n)}m,
\]

and the circular projection:

\[
C_m(n)=\left(\cos\theta_m(n),\sin\theta_m(n)\right).
\]

The multi-axis curve is therefore:

\[
\Gamma(n)=\left(n,C_{m_1}(n),\ldots,C_{m_k}(n)\right).
\]

The `n` coordinate is never erased when one modular coordinate becomes zero.
