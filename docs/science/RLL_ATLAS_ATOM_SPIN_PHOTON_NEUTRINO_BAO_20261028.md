## 1. Atomic-to-BAO links: established mechanisms versus inference
**Spectroscopic line route:** chemical element atomic number Z, charge state q, electronic level and transition determine a rest-frame wavelength; for a correctly identified absorption line, `z = lambda_obs/lambda_rest - 1`. The optical spectra of distant quasars sample intergalactic absorbers. Through the 3D correlation function, these become BAO tracers. **This does not mean the atomic transition creates the BAO acoustic ruler.** It provides a precise observation of its large-scale spatial correlations.

A particularly direct modern connection: [DESI DR2 C IV × QSO/ELG (arXiv:2601.08103)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08103). C IV = triply ionized carbon (carbon Z=6, 3 remaining bound electrons); doublet rest wavelengths **1548.20 Å and 1550.78 Å**. Authors' BAO results: `D_V/r_d(z_eff=1.92)=30.3 +/- 0.9` (C IV×QSO, 4.2 sigma), and `D_V/r_d(z_eff=1.47)=24.6 +/- 1.0` (C IV×ELG, 2.5 sigma). Measurement claims belong solely to the source study; paper's tracer selection, covariance and metal contamination must be audited before reuse. Official DESI DR2 July 2026 papers are indexed at https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/papers/dr2/ . The DR2 spectra/redshifts are not yet generally released as of this checkpoint even where chains/BAO summaries are available.

**Primordial plasma route:** `Gamma_T = n_e sigma_T c` [s^-1] for photon Thomson scattering rate, with free electron density `n_e` [m^-3], cross-section `sigma_T` [m^2]. Ionization/recombination sets photon-baryon coupling and visibility; the baryon-drag sound horizon `r_d = integral_{z_d}^{infinity} c_s(z)/H(z) dz` [length, with `c_s` in physical speed units and `H` 1/s]. Coupling requires full perturbation and recombination physics, not only a scalar logistic sigmoid. Atomic energy/electron configuration enters radiative transfer and recombination rates but is not equivalent to dark-energy dynamics.

**Photon transfer:** `E_gamma = h nu = hc/lambda`; in a measured transition `Delta E = h nu`, with selection rules, oscillator strength, level population and instrumental response. Atomic line identification and level energies come from [NIST ASD v5.12](https://www.nist.gov/pml/atomic-spectra-database); atomic number, standard atomic weight and ground-state configuration from [NIST Periodic Table](https://www.nist.gov/pml/periodic-table-elements). Atomic *weight* is not isotope number A, and either is not a statistical likelihood weight.

## 2. Geometric spin experiment and photonic interactions
[Observation of electron spin interactions between Rydberg atoms (2026, arXiv:2608.23711)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23711) reports two-atom `87Rb` electron-spin exchange at `d=9.66 μm` and external magnetic field `21.7 G`. At `theta=0 deg`, `J_xy/h=0.236 +/- 0.002 MHz` as reported. Its angular dependence is modeled by `J_xy(theta)/J = (5+3cos(2theta))/8` and `J_z(theta)/J=(1-3cos(2theta))/4` for the stated XXZ approximation. Therefore `J_xy(90deg)/J_xy(0deg) = 1/4` **as a prediction of this specific model** at the same distance and settings, not a new measurement or cosmological force law. Error bars in the paper's Fig. 2 are 2-sigma intervals as described there.

[2026 Rydberg-mediated nonlinear quantum optics review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02992) surveys blockade, electromagnetically induced transparency, polaritons, photon-photon effective interactions and quantum photonic gates. Both studies involve Rydberg systems, but their experimental samples/protocols are not interchangeable. A joint spin-to-photon transduction claim requires a device-specific experiment, optical mode and coherence/readout calibration.

Spin-1/2 electron `s`, orbital angular momentum `l`, total `j`, magnetic projection `m_j`, photon spin/helicity and neutrino helicity refer to different representations/interactions. Common angular momentum units do **not** justify equating their dynamics. State weights `g_J=2J+1` apply to unsplit multiplets, not to every Zeeman-split component.

## 3. Neutrinos: link to BAO phase, not to PeV events by analogy
[Whitford et al., JCAP March 2026, arXiv:2511.00800](https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2026/03/064) discusses the neutrino free-streaming acoustic phase; changes from self-interactions are forecast using CMB+BAO. This is a real field-theoretic bridge via cosmological perturbations. The study does **not** establish a new interaction detection, and forecasts BAO alone to be insufficiently precise for strong coupling constraints. The `N_eff=3.044` Standard-Model baseline and neutrino mass priors must be explicit, and neutrino anisotropic stress cannot be replaced by a background-only `a^-4` term.

For neutrino↔atom: [radiative emission of a neutrino pair (RENP), arXiv:2306.12953](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12953) is a proposed weak-interaction route `atom* -> atom + photon + nu + anti-nu` to study electromagnetic form factors. **Theory/feasibility proposal only**, not a confirmed event sample. For mass-varying neutrino dark-energy models, [arXiv:2609.25090](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25090) reports model/prior-dependent constraints with no consistently preferred case relative to flat ΛCDM. [arXiv:2606.21518](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21518) stresses the difference between fully-thermalized and suppressed-abundance sterile neutrinos.

## 4. Model comparison falsifier: an observational block can dominate
[Karim, Speagle, Hlozek, arXiv:2609.04334 (September 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04334) examine leave-one-redshift-block-out expected log predictive density (ELPD). In **their analysis**, the BAO predictive advantage for w0waCDM is concentrated in **LRG2, z=0.706**; SNIa blocks do not exhibit the same advantage. This is a valuable independent-replication target, **not** general proof the entire DESI anomaly comes from one tracer. Avoid a naive leave-one-data-row-out when covariance couples observables. Predeclare the block selection and compare RLL, flat ΛCDM, wCDM, CPL using matched physical priors and model complexity.

DESI full source: https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/papers/dr2/ . Evolving dark-energy analysis in August 2026 [MNRAS stag1607](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stag1607) reports ~3-sigma frequentist preference but not decisive Bayesian evidence, reminding that `delta_chi2 != Bayes_factor != delta_ELPD`.
