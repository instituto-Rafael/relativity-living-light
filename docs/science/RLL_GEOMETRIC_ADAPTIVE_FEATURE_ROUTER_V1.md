## 8. Compass / mirror / puzzle methods

The useful mathematical interpretation is operational:

- compass: construct exact equal-radius, chord and intersection constraints;
- mirror: test reflection/orientation equivalence;
- puzzle: decompose a complex figure into exact local constraints, then reconstruct;
- rotate/overlay: search invariants under a declared group action;
- inversion: exchange near/far scale while preserving (rr'=a^2).

These are valid *methods of generating falsifiable geometry fixtures*. They do not bypass the physical model.
